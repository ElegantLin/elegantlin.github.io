Abstract
---
>Although the unprecedented development of the Internet has brought many benefits, it also brings the challenge of Internet Addiction Disorder (IAD) in our society, which is hard to diagnose and cure according to the state-of-art research. The existing works mainly focus on a single factor in statistical methods. One of the distinctive aspects of this study is that we investigated several factors and explored the feasibility of machine learning methods to detect IAD. We created a dataset from 3123 Chinese college students from Tongji University (M<sub>age</sub> = 19.18, SD = 0.70, 64.2% are males) who completed several questionnaires including Brief Self Control Scale (BSCS), the 11th version of Barratt Impulsiveness Scale (BIS-11), Chinese Big Five Personality Inventory (CBF-PI) and Chen Internet Addiction Scale (CIAS). We applied Student’s t-test on the dataset for feature selection and Support Vector Machine (SVM) with grid search for the classification and the optimization of the parameters. This work illustrates that SVM is a reliable method for assessment of IAD and questionnaire data analysis. The best detection performance is 96.3% which was obtained by C-SVM in 6-feature dataset without normalization. The BIS, BSCS, Action, Neuroticism, Non-planning and Rigor are shown to be better detection features than the others.
