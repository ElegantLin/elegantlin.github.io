Zonglin Di is an EECS undergraduate student in Tongji University, where he has covered the courses of Control Science, Computer Science and part of Software Engineering. You can find my CV [here](/files/Zonglin Di CV.pdf). During his undergraduate experience, he gradually found his interests in **Bio-computation in machine learning**, **imbalanced classification** and **road extraction using deep learning and feature engineering** supervised by [Prof. Asoke Nandi](http://www.brunel.ac.uk/people/asoke-k-nandi), [Prof. Qi Kang](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=t8K4P4wAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN) & [Prof. Mengchu Zhou](https://web.njit.edu/~zhou/) and [Prof. Yin Wang](http://web.eecs.umich.edu/~yinw/), respectively. He is now trying using **Deep Learning** methods to solve the problems using traditional Machine Learning methods. In addition to research, he is also interested in Engineering during his 4 internship experiences to make the magic of research and technology into practice. Now he serves as a **full-stack software intern** to develop an **Auto-Build System** for [National Instruments](http://www.ni.com/en-us.html) R & D, Shanghai, China. As for social activities in technical area, he serves as **head of Virtual Instruments Club, Tongji University** from 2016 sponsored by National Instruments (an important branch of NI Academic Program in Great China Area) and established the **Open Source Hardware Club, Tongji University** in 2017 sponsored by [Digilent Inc.](http://www.digilent.com.cn/). From Jan. 2017, he began his volunteer in [Google Developer Group, Shanghai](https://chinagdg.org/p/gdg-shanghai/).
