Abstract
---
>Imbalance classification is a common but critical problem in machine learning, artificial intelligence and the area related. Although the traditional classification method Support Vector Machine (SVM) enjoys great reputation in classification, it fails to take the distribution of the data into consideration and leads to some loss in the performance of classification to some extend. However, if the distribution information among the classes can be given to the classification process first, the classifier should perform better. Recently, Adaptive Clustering by Fast Search and Find of Density Peaks (ADPclust) was proposed and performs well in finding the cluster centroid in the sample space automatically and more reliably using adaptive density peak detection and silhouette theory. We combine the ADPclust and SVM to retain the information of the original data point distribution and propose a new method called ADPclust-SVM, which pre-classifies the dataset into several groups and applies under-sampling in the following SVM classification step if there is an imbalance problem among the pre-clustered groups. After experiments on 23 binary public datasets and evaluation using Area Under Curve (AUC), F-Measure, G-Mean, ADPclust shows its superiority over SVM, Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique (SMOTE) algorithm and Undersampling SVM (U-SVM), which implies its effectiveness in dealing with binary imbalance classification problem.
